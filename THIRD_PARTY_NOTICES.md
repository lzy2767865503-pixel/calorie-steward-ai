## USDA FoodData Central

The historical food-catalogue experiment in `data/` and `android-app/app/src/main/assets/databases/clinical_clarity_foods.sqlite` contains selected records derived from USDA FoodData Central. FoodData Central data are made available under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal public-domain dedication. The repository preserves source-release and nutrient provenance in the dataset manifest.

`data/` 和 `android-app/app/src/main/assets/databases/clinical_clarity_foods.sqlite` 中的历史食品库实验包含源自 USDA FoodData Central 的筛选记录。FoodData Central 数据以 Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal 公共领域贡献方式提供。仓库在 dataset manifest 中保留源版本和营养素溯源。

- USDA licensing statement: <https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/>
- Source documentation: <https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/data-documentation.html>

The catalogue is a nutrient reference. It does not prove which food, recipe or portion appears in a photograph, and it does not establish Malaysian shelf availability.
